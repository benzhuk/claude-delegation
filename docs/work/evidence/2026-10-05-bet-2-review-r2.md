### R2-1 MEDIUM, sight, regression against main: the N5 decrypt rule passes stdout and `-noout -text`

Evidence: guard:714. The exemption `[[:space:]](-out|-noout|-o|--output)([[:space:]=]|$)|[^0-9&>]>...` treats any `-out`, `-o` or `--output` as "writes a file". `-` means stdout for openssl, age, rage and gpg. `>` with no space before `/dev/stdout` counts as a file redirect, and the N4 sink rule needs whitespace or `=` before the device, so it misses that form too. `-noout` exempts `openssl rsa|pkey -text`, which prints the modulus, privateExponent and both primes, which together are the whole private key. Probes S01-S06b and S56 above: main denies all seven, 589d3ef allows them.

Fix: insert two checks before guard:714, and let a trailing `-noout` match before the JSON closing quote. The exact text is in the patch at the end. Predicted and measured: S01-S06b and S56 deny. F05 `openssl x509 -in cert.pem -noout -fingerprint`, F06 `-out /tmp/b.pem`, F07 `-noout -modulus`, F24 `-text -out /tmp/b.txt`, F25 `-pubout -out`, F12 `sops -d -o file`, F13 `age -e ... -o`, G07 `age -d ... -o /tmp/plain` and G06 `openssl rsa -in K -check -noout` allow. G06 is denied on 589d3ef today, because a trailing `-noout` is followed by `"` in the payload, not by a space. That is R2-6, fixed by the same patch.

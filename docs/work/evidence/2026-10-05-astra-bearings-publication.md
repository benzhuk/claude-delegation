	Decision: RE-PLAN for the inherited harness-improvement direction. Complete the already authorized no-code method bet with a concrete useful-task trial proposal. Neither proposed architecture is ready to implement.
	Scope: September 30 through October 5, 2026, America/New_York. The assessment used the goal, prior predictions, work records, a raw-evidence audit and the owner's recorded clarification. The older card still displayed on this page has not been rewritten by this assessment.
	## What the evidence says
	- Significant progress: delivery and actual component removals are verified, but improvement in useful work at equal quality is not established.
	- Sidelined: recurring internal census, publishing, guard and cleanup work occupies the evidence while the underlying problem remains unsettled. Some of this work was explicitly authorized and necessary.
	- Patches instead of structural correction: owner-input and state-location failures recur after local repairs. That warrants examining their shared boundaries, not declaring the entire repository waste.
	- Simplest useful solution: unproven. Fewer files or machines can transfer work to the owner or remove required behavior.
	## What matters next
	- First, align acceptance with the owner's clarified purpose. Otherwise every local improvement can still optimize the wrong result.
	- Second, obtain comparable evidence from a real project task, including required quality and owner attention. Existing output counts do not establish benefit.
	- Third, explain recurring failures across state and authority boundaries before another repair there.
	- Fourth, close or explicitly supersede earlier predictions. The October 1 cost prediction is unresolved in this packet, not counted as passed.
	## Lead response
	I accept RE-PLAN. The completed method review identifies unsupported failure-count claims, dependencies proposed for removal, missing replacement protections and unpriced manual work. The next pitch should recommend one already wanted development task, its observable result, one suspected structural failure and a bounded comparison using existing tools. Preserve required protection and recovery behavior during that trial.
	The owner decides value, appetite and acceptable losses. The lead supplies a technical recommendation and preserves the objections. There is no need to repeat approval of the current no-code bet. This assessment authorizes no deletion, guard change, host shutdown, implementation or merge.
	## Prediction and stop accounting
	By October 6 at 5:00 PM America/New_York, one decision record should explain the change from the old card, name retained benefits, identify a real task with acceptance and rejection criteria, and give a causal argument for one claimed failure-class reduction. 	## Evidence and limits
	The audit reproduced 3,246 tracked paths at the reviewed base, about 90 percent under documentation. It also verified actual component removals. These facts establish accumulation, not that every file is a patch or that every protection should disappear.
	The proposed four failure families sum to 197 rows, not 218. Rows overlap and mix different kinds of observations. The retained loop also depends on acceptance and census helpers proposed for removal. These direct checks support revising the designs; no proposed replacement was executed or tested.

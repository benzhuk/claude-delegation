# Independent research: improve the investment decision, then the delivery

Completed October 5, 2026, America/New_York. Historical filename retained from the brief. This synthesis was written before reading the incumbent proposals. It is an evidence review, not proof of a complete method.

The strongest common lesson is to connect the next expenditure to a useful outcome and to the uncertainty most likely to invalidate it. Preserve enough behavioral evidence and coherent design to change direction safely. Neither more approvals, perpetual incremental repair, nor wholesale replacement is a generally justified answer. The familiar methods largely offer experienced judgment and plausible mechanisms; they do not establish the optimal process for Ben and transient agents.

Reports distinguish substantive reading from snippets and failed retrievals. - [Agents, judgment and measurement](2026-10-05-astra-agents-research.md): controlled productivity studies, vendor engineering accounts, multi-agent failure analysis, correlated judgment and evaluation cost. No claim that agreement between agents establishes truth.

It overlapped external research after the initial problem statement; the detailed audit was not complete at that first commit.

This is a purposive review, not a systematic search or an exhaustive reading of the packet's author list. No Humphrey, Peopleware, Gall, Alexander, Polya, Cockburn, Cagan or SAFe primary work was substantively assessed. Gause/Weinberg and Beck books, Toyota's substantive evidence and some experiments cited by other authors remain unread. The limits are material: I cannot claim to have disproved those approaches. Another broad literature sweep is unlikely to select the next investment better than examining one actual project outcome.

## Patterns worth using, with their costs

| Problem | Transfer worth trying | Evidence strength and cost |
|---|---|---|
| Requirements are mistaken for a settled problem | Start from the user's work and the assumption that could make the proposed work worthless; try a cheap discriminating observation | Conceptual and experience-based discovery literature. Interviews and probes consume owner time and can still ask the wrong question. |
| Local fixes conceal a structural mistake | Trace changes and failures across state ownership and dependency boundaries; propose one bounded correction with a retirement condition | Parnas, Fowler and Shopify provide mechanisms and cases, not an agent-project trial. Migration and temporary dual operation can cost more than the repair. |
| Planning consumes unlimited commitment | Set an appetite for the next useful slice; renew explicitly if the question changes | Shape Up experience, Boehm risk reasoning. A deadline can hide scope loss; neither method proves the work is useful. |
| Owner approval substitutes for expertise | Ben sets value and tolerable losses; a responsible technical lead recommends using observable evidence, escalating genuine value tradeoffs | Governance documents and expertise research synthesis. Expertise and direct checks still cost time; a council of models does not solve calibration. |
| Review becomes a backlog of preferences | Review observable behavior, material risk and overall maintainability; separate blocking defects from optional polish | Google practice and DORA associations. Removing approval layers does not authorize removing security boundaries or behavior checks. |
| A rewrite discards knowledge | Preserve externally required behavior and replace through a tractable seam, retiring the old path | Mixed practitioner evidence: Stripe and Fowler support incremental migration; Netflix illustrates circumstances requiring major redesign. No universal rewrite prohibition follows. |
| Agents manufacture apparent agreement | Separate initial judgments, require evidence outside their prose, and use a discriminating executable or user observation | Correlation and self-preference experiments support caution; transfer from benchmark judgments to code review remains an inference. More reviewers add latency and correlated cost. |
| Cheap generation conceals expensive supervision | Measure total work to useful acceptance, including owner attention, reviews, waits, repairs and maintenance | Controlled productivity evidence is heterogeneous. Vendor multi-agent stories are not a guarantee for coding. Counting generated tasks or files is inadequate. |
| Session turnover loses intent | Keep a concise current contract, actual state and next action in existing durable artifacts | Documentation and harness experience; largely untested as a combined method. Stale duplicate summaries can worsen the original problem. |

## Disagreement in the evidence matters

The productivity literature does not justify either “AI always accelerates development” or “AI slows developers.” The studies examine different developers, tools, tasks and outcomes. METR's early experienced-maintainer experiment found a slowdown; a later update describes selection and measurement problems that prevent a clean current estimate. Enterprise completion-tool trials measured increased task output in other settings. None tests this plugin's mixed-host handoff. These limitations are detailed in the agent report; they are a reason to measure the actual workflow, not to choose the study with the preferred sign.

Similarly, a successful migration is not proof that a rewrite is generally wise, and a rewrite disaster is not proof that every old state model deserves indefinite preservation. Shopify's own account shows why extra interfaces can add indirection without reducing dependency complexity. The correct comparison includes the transitional and recurring costs of both alternatives.

The organizational documents explain how mature communities allocate authority; they do not demonstrate that recreating their committees among disposable agents helps a single owner. Copy the useful question—who is responsible and what evidence changes the decision—without copying the institution.

## What the local evidence changes

At the audited revision, 2,924 of 3,246 tracked paths are documentation. There are 141 direct work records, including 82 accepted, and 84 test files. Those are not 82 independent features or 84 test cases. The net growth is real, but “all growth is runtime machinery” is false. The census/pickup/loop counts in earlier material are overlapping keyword hits, not mutually exclusive failures. Actual component removals are documented; “the project never deletes anything” is false too. See the raw audit for exact revisions and counting methods.

The important deficiency is stronger and narrower: these outputs do not establish improvement in useful delivery, owner burden, or the goal's four comparable measures. The recorded stalled work, inaccurate clock, publication divergence and ownership visibility failures support examining the design and decision process. They do not identify a single root cause or prove that every retained protection is waste.

## Decision implications

1. Choose a useful external project result before funding another harness capability. A harness demonstration may still be a release condition, but cannot stand in for project value.
2. Define the required behavior and intolerable losses before choosing what to remove. Retire a route only after its necessary responsibility is demonstrably preserved or explicitly relinquished by the owner.
3. Use the current tools to run a bounded comparison. Record raw numerator, denominator, timing boundaries and unknowns. A small case study can falsify a bad mechanism; it cannot establish population-wide superiority.
4. Give one technical lead responsibility for a coherent recommendation. Use independent review at consequential decisions, with distinct evidence or adversarial cases, rather than requiring a fixed panel on every edit.
5. Cap research and process work. Each additional artifact or check must resolve a named uncertainty or prevent an evidenced loss at a cost worth paying. This research itself is a one-time investment, not a template for ordinary builds.

These are my inferences across the source records. No read source validates this package as a whole or establishes the right number of agents, meetings, pages or days.
